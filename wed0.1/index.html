How it all began

Written in the stars, sealed with chai

Arjun and Kavya first crossed paths at a mutual friend's Diwali gathering in Bengaluru, 2021. He was the one awkwardly holding two plates of sweets; she was the one who offered to hold one. That exchange of a plate of ladoos turned into hours of conversation under string lights, and neither of them wanted the evening to end.

What followed were long phone calls between Bengaluru and Bengaluru, weekend train journeys, and the slow, beautiful realisation that they were building something rare. When Arjun proposed on the terrace of the same friend's home two years later — on another Diwali, lit with the same lights — Kavya said yes before he'd finished the sentence.
