I started as an Android developer, building consumer apps and learning what it means to ship something people actually use. That foundation shaped how I think about software: reliability, performance, and user trust matter.

I moved into DevOps and infrastructure, learning to build the systems that keep software running — CI/CD, containerisation, orchestration. That's when I realised the most valuable thing I could do was make data move reliably between systems at scale.
